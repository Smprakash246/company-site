What goes wrong

Where clinics lose patients online.

  1. Slow or outdated website

    Patients leave before they find your timings or phone number.

  2. Ads that reach the wrong people

    Budget spent on clicks from across the city, not your neighbourhood.

  3. Weak Google Maps listing

    Missing photos, wrong hours, few recent reviews.

  4. No follow-up after treatment

    Recalls are missed and patients drift to another clinic.

  5. No clear reporting

    You don't know what the agency did or what it brought in.
