Questions

What dentists ask us

Does the AI give clinical advice?

No. It shares your dentist's aftercare instructions and general tooth-care tips, and sends anything serious to your team. It never diagnoses or prescribes.

Is it built for dental practices?

Yes. Its aftercare, recalls and alerts are designed around dental treatments: extractions, root canals, fillings, hygiene, orthodontics, implants and cosmetic work.

Do patients need to download an app?

No. Everything happens on WhatsApp, which your patients already use.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Polish, Romanian, Spanish, Portuguese, Urdu, Punjabi, Arabic and more. It replies in the patient's language.

What happens at night when the practice is closed?

The AI answers general questions, offers the first slot next morning, and for dental emergencies gives your emergency line or NHS 111 straight away.

What does reception have to do?

Add patients after treatment (or let them scan the QR card), and reply to alerts. A short reply like "4:30 PM" is enough; the AI confirms with the patient.

Can we add our existing patients?

Yes. We import them from a spreadsheet during setup, and each patient accepts the terms before the AI starts.

What about patient data?

Each practice sees only its own patients. Patients agree to the terms first, can stop messages any time with STOP, and can ask for their data to be deleted.
