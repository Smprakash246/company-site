How it works

Four steps. Your doctor only does the first one.

1

The doctor recommends it

"Chat with our clinic's AI on this number. It'll help you until your next visit. You can type in Tamil." When the doctor says it, patients try it.

2

The nurse adds the patient

Name, WhatsApp number, treatment, medicines and timings, next visit. Or the patient scans the QR board. The patient accepts the terms with one tap.

3

The AI cares, 24 hours

Care tips, medicine reminders, answers in the patient's language, two options when there's a problem, follow-up checks, festival wishes.

4

Your team gets the alerts

Appointment requests, new leads and recalls arrive on the nurse's WhatsApp with a short summary. She replies "5 PM", the AI confirms with the patient.

Questions

What doctors ask us

Does the AI give medical advice?

No. It shares your doctor's care instructions and general care tips, reminds medicines exactly as prescribed, and sends anything serious to your team. It never diagnoses or changes a dose.

Is it only for dental clinics?

No. It works for any clinic or hospital whose patients need care between visits: dental, diabetes, general practice, ortho, maternity, eye, skin, heart and multi-speciality.

Do patients need to download an app?

No. Everything happens on WhatsApp, which your patients already use.

Which languages does it speak?

Tamil, Tanglish, English, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada, including the casual way people type. It replies in the patient's language.

What happens at night when the clinic is closed?

The AI answers general questions, offers the first slot next morning, and for real emergencies gives your emergency number or the nearest hospital straight away.

What does my nurse have to do?

Add patients after treatment (or let them scan the QR board), and reply to alerts. A short reply like "5 PM" is enough; the AI confirms with the patient.

Can we add our existing patients?

Yes. We import them from Excel during setup, and each patient accepts the terms before the AI starts.

What if someone sends junk or tries to misuse it?

The AI stays on health and clinic topics. It replies with one polite line and then stays silent, so your WhatsApp costs stay low.
