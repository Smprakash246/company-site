Privacy and safety

Built for patient trust.

Do patients agree before the AI talks to them?

Yes. Every patient accepts simple terms on WhatsApp first. Sharing health notes is a separate choice.

Can a patient stop messages?

Yes. Replying STOP stops all messages for that number at once. START turns them back on.

Does it give medical advice?

No. It shares aftercare text approved by your doctors, never diagnoses, and sends anything serious to your team.

Who owns the patient data?

Your clinic. Each clinic's records are kept separate, and patients can ask for their data to be deleted.

Does the clinic need new hardware?

No. Your staff use the clinic app on any phone or computer.
