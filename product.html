Questions

Things clinics ask us.

Do patients need to install an app?

No. Everything happens on WhatsApp, which they already use.

Which languages does it speak?

Tamil, English and Tanglish. It replies in the language the patient uses.

Does it give medical advice?

No. It shares the general aftercare instructions your doctors approve, and sends anything serious straight to your team.

Can our staff take over a chat?

Yes. Your front desk can reply from the same WhatsApp number at any time.

How long does setup take?

About one to two weeks, including writing the messages with your doctors.
